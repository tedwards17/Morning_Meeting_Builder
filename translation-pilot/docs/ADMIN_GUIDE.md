# Administrator and content-manager guide

1. Sign in with the unique first administrator account. Confirm organization name/timezone and Readiness.
2. In Company workspace → Templates, install the DTB starter if the company is empty. Replace SAMPLE content with approved material before regular shop use.
3. Locations starts with Assembly, Paint, Mount, and Detail. Disable unused locations or create more. Create one fixed location account per meeting location; a location/category cannot be reassigned later. Use unique 12+ character passwords. Multiple browsers may sign into the same account, each with its own device UUID/session.
4. Create individual administrator, content-manager, or report-viewer accounts. Administrators manage identity/security/settings. Content managers maintain content/broadcasts and templates they own or may edit. Report viewers cannot write content or administer accounts. An explicit template-creation capability can be granted independently; DTB location accounts default to enabled.
5. Build libraries/content in the original Content library screen. Hosted uploads accept JPEG, PNG, WebP. Image-with-text adds a caption/body. Use Online video for YouTube or Link for another web page. Direct video uploads and PDFs are not hosted V2 inputs. Content scope is set in Company workspace → Libraries/Content scope: no target locations means company-wide; otherwise only selected locations. Library and content scope are both enforced.
6. Templates: create/edit the master in the original Templates screen. In Company workspace → Templates manage company use, account/location use and edit permissions, and named slots on slides. Sharing is owner/full-administrator-only; editable does not automatically mean usable. Master saves create immutable versions. Daily drafts are snapshots and do not change masters.
7. Broadcasts: choose library content, schedule start/end in the organization timezone, select once-per-location or every-meeting, choose available/suggested/required, priority, slot, and targets. Use Preview to inspect locations and slot/fallback placement before setting Published. For a one-date broadcast use 00:00–23:59. Higher priorities come first, then earlier start, creation sequence, UUID tie-breaker. Missing slots fall back after Introduction; without Introduction they are prepended. Suggested/available appear as explicit builder choices. Required content inserts automatically and cannot be removed/edited in the draft. Presentation skips require a reason.
8. Reports provide required/broadcast completion, skips/reasons, location activity, events, safety-video usage, content use, and meeting snapshots. Export CSV; formula-looking strings are neutralized. Historical events retain titles/versions. A required slide that opened but was subsequently skipped is reported as skipped and remains due for once-per-location scheduling. YouTube player state provides start/completion only when the embedded player reports it; opening an external page alone is not claimed as video completion.
9. Devices lists each installation, last seen/sync, and revocation. Revoking one device invalidates its sessions without revoking other devices at that location. Offline copies cannot be remotely erased; use device lock/management for lost tablets.
10. Storage lists images by size/type/archive state. Archive before attempting deletion. Deletion is blocked if any current record, immutable record version, or meeting references the image. GD/WebP optimization and thumbnail creation are used when supported; otherwise original validated files remain protected.

Edits to company content/templates require online access. On a 409 conflict, retain/copy your intended change, Sync Now, reopen the latest record, and apply the change again. The API never silently overwrites a newer version. Meeting drafts and events can remain offline in the outbox.
